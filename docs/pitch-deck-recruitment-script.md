# True Fit: presentation script, the recruitment process (5 min, 8 slides)

> A slide-by-slide script for a PowerPoint pitch. Each slide has **what goes on the slide** (keep it short) and **what to say** (paste into the Notes pane).
> Audience: non-technical judges and City of Espoo staff.
> Story: **the problem → skills instead of CVs → the six steps → why it is fair → what it achieves**.
> About 650 spoken words. The steps and rules come straight from the app (`lib/pipeline.ts`, `lib/schedule.ts`). Every person in the demo is synthetic.

## Slide 1 · The problem (35 s)

**On the slide**

- **True Fit**: more signal, less noise in recruitment
- AI writes the CVs. AI screens the CVs. Everyone looks perfect on paper.
- Who gets lost: career changers, newcomers, people without a polished Finnish CV

**Say**

> Hi, we're True Fit. Today anyone can generate a perfect CV in seconds, and companies run it through an AI filter. So the CV says less and less about what someone can actually do.
> The people who lose out are the ones Espoo most wants to help: career changers, newcomers to Finland, anyone whose skills don't fit a template.
> So we asked: at Espoo's recruitment events, how do you make sure the right people meet, and that it's fair?

---

## Slide 2 · Skills, not CVs (45 s)

**On the slide**

- **Job seeker:** job titles or CV → skills from the EU skills list → four real-life questions → skills **shown**, with their own words as proof
- **Company:** a role card, written by chatting or speaking with an assistant
- **Match = skills only.** No name, nationality, language or school.

**Say**

> First, both sides describe themselves in the same language: skills.
> A job seeker types past job titles or uploads a CV, and we turn that into skills from the EU's official skills list. Then they answer four everyday questions, and the AI points out the skills their answers actually show, quoting their own sentence as proof. They check every claim themselves.
> A company describes the role by chatting or speaking with an assistant.
> The match uses skills and nothing else. Our tests fail if it ever looks at nationality, language, education or background.

---

## Slide 3 · One pipeline, six steps (25 s)

**On the slide**

1. Applied or matched → 2. Espoo assessment → 3. Espoo interview → 4. Profile sent to company → 5. Company interview → 6. Job offer

**Every step is a human decision. The skill match only suggests.**

**Say**

> Then every company role and candidate move through the same six steps, with the City of Espoo as the trusted broker in the middle.
> At every step the app shows where things stand and whose move it is. And every step is decided by a person.

---

## Slide 4 · Steps 1–2: ask and assess (40 s)

**On the slide**

- **1 · Applied or matched:** the candidate clicks **Apply now**, or the company clicks **Ask Espoo Esbo to introduce**
- **2 · Espoo assessment:** skill match + evidence + optional AI fit rate → **invite to an Espoo interview** or **pass for now**, always with a reason
- Nobody is rejected automatically

**Say**

> It starts when the candidate applies, or the company asks Espoo to introduce someone. Asking first never changes the score.
> Then Espoo reviews the pair: which skills match, the candidate's own words as evidence, and if it wants, an AI fit rate with a short reason. Espoo invites the candidate to a short interview or passes for now, always with a written reason that goes into a log. The machine never filters anyone out.

---

## Slide 5 · Steps 3–4: meet Espoo first (40 s)

**On the slide**

- **3 · Espoo interview:** the candidate accepts and books a slot (Otaniemi or online). Espoo records **passed / not passed**, with a reason.
- **4 · Profile sent to company:** **only after a pass** does the company see the profile and contact details

**Say**

> The candidate accepts the invitation and books a time at the Espoo desk, in person or online. Afterwards Espoo records whether they passed, again with a reason.
> Only then does the company see the full profile and contact details. That protects candidates' privacy, and companies only spend time on people Espoo has already met. That is the real signal a CV can't give you.

---

## Slide 6 · Steps 5–6: interview and offer (40 s)

**On the slide**

- **5 · Company interview:** **both sides must say yes**. Either side books a slot, never before the Espoo interview and never clashing. Both give quick feedback.
- **6 · Job offer:** the company offers or not, the candidate accepts or declines → **Hired** ✅

**Say**

> The company decides whether to interview, and the candidate has to say yes too. A meeting only happens when both want it.
> Either side books the interview. The calendar blocks clashes and times before the Espoo interview. Afterwards both sides rate the meeting and how accurate the cards were, never the person.
> Finally the company makes an offer and the candidate accepts or declines. Accepted means hired.

---

## Slide 7 · Fair by design (45 s)

**On the slide**

- **People decide.** The AI suggests and explains, nothing more.
- **Skills only**, and tested
- **Every decision has a reason**, kept in a log
- **Undo clears later steps**, so nobody acts on old information
- **Fairness check** across nationality, language, education and career path
- **Espoo's view:** every pair, whose move it is, and live event insights

**Say**

> Fairness is built into the steps, not added at the end. People decide everything. Matching uses skills only, and we test it. Every decision has a reason. If Espoo undoes a decision, every later step is cleared. And nobody is told why the other side said no.
> We also compare match rates across nationality, language, education and career path, and explain how this would meet the EU AI Act.
> Espoo sees every pair in one place, who needs to act next, and how the event is going.

---

## Slide 8 · Impact (30 s)

**On the slide**

- **Job seekers:** noticed for what they can do
- **Companies:** fewer meetings, better fits, each one met by Espoo first
- **Espoo:** a fair, visible process from first click to job offer
- **True Fit: more signal, less noise.**

**Say**

> So what changes? Job seekers get noticed for what they can actually do. Companies meet fewer, better-matched people that Espoo has already met. And Espoo runs a fair, visible process from the first click to the job offer, with people deciding every step.
> True Fit: more signal, less noise. Thank you!
